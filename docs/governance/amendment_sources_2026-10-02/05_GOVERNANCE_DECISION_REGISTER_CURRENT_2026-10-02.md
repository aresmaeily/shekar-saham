## Additive owner-directed decisions — 2026-10-02

### D-AI-32 — Continuous Market and External-Source Monitoring
**CURRENT OWNER-DIRECTED PRODUCT REQUIREMENT**

The final product must support governed scheduled and event-triggered monitoring of the market, approved watchlists and a versioned source universe beyond user-prompted requests. Coverage must be explicit and auditable; universal coverage of every web URL must not be claimed.

### D-AI-33 — Verified Company/Fundamental Intelligence
**CURRENT OWNER-DIRECTED PRODUCT REQUIREMENT**

Company dossiers and fundamental analysis must combine point-in-time official filings, financial statements, audit/disclosure information and approved corroborating sources. Every fact retains source, publication time, reporting period, units/currency, version/restatement state, trust and conflict status. Missing or contradictory data is surfaced, not invented or silently reconciled.

### D-AI-34 — TabloKhani-Style Reference Benchmark and Evidence-Based Extension
**CURRENT OWNER-DIRECTED PRODUCT REQUIREMENT**

TabloKhani-style market-board interpretation is the initial reference benchmark. The product must preserve relevant behavior while extending it through validated multi-source intelligence, fundamentals, regime, risk, research memory and evaluated findings. Superiority claims require a versioned Golden Set and repeatable benchmark evidence.

### D-AI-35 — AI Product Intelligence and Improvement Loop
**CURRENT OWNER-DIRECTED PRODUCT REQUIREMENT**

The final product must periodically analyze privacy-safe product-quality/UX/usage signals, filter false positives, feature adoption where lawfully available, reliability, data quality and AI output quality. It may propose improvements with baseline, evidence, uncertainty, expected impact, risk and evaluation/rollback plan. It may not mutate production or release changes.

### D-AI-36 — Whole-Product Periodic Evaluation
**CURRENT OWNER-DIRECTED PRODUCT REQUIREMENT**

The product must have recurring, bounded audits across financial filters/strategies, market/fundamental AI, data/provider operations, UX, performance, security and reliability. Use backtest/WFO/forward test only for appropriate financial hypotheses; use domain-appropriate regression/performance/security/UX tests for software/product components.

### D-AI-37 — Prediction-to-Outcome Feedback and Calibration
**CURRENT OWNER-DIRECTED PRODUCT REQUIREMENT**

Predictions and opportunity hypotheses must be timestamped before outcomes, linked to predefined outcome horizons and point-in-time evidence, then evaluated after outcomes mature. Error analysis, calibration and drift may generate research proposals but cannot directly change production logic.

### D-AI-38 — Coverage, Freshness and Source Access Honesty
**CURRENT OWNER-DIRECTED PRODUCT REQUIREMENT**

All monitoring outputs must state source coverage, last successful retrieval, freshness, known gaps, source failures and unresolved conflicts. Respect permitted access, rate limits, privacy and applicable source policies. Do not imply universal web coverage.

### D-AI-39 — Privacy-Safe Product Telemetry
**CURRENT OWNER-DIRECTED PRODUCT REQUIREMENT**

Product Intelligence uses minimum necessary, access-controlled, privacy-safe telemetry. No sensitive user content is repurposed without a lawful, approved basis. Aggregation, retention, access and deletion rules must be documented before telemetry-driven improvement is accepted.

### D-AI-40 — Existing Governance Gates Remain Binding
**PRESERVED / REAFFIRMED**

D-AI-01 through D-AI-31 remain in force. G2 still blocks real-data AI-2 execution until independent validation and owner acceptance. External intelligence remains untrusted and gated. No live trading, production mutation, auto-promotion, self-escalation or self-modifying production logic is authorized by D-AI-32 through D-AI-39.

### D-AI-41 — Product Capability Is Not Implementation Evidence
**CURRENT GOVERNANCE RULE**

Adding these requirements to the roadmap does not upgrade implementation status. Monitoring, fundamental intelligence, product audits, feedback loops and benchmark superiority remain NOT PROVEN until the corresponding acceptance evidence exists.
