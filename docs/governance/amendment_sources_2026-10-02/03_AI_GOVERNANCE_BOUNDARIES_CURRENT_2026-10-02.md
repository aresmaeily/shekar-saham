# RADAR_BOURSE --- Final AI Governance Boundaries

Revision: 2026-09-29 Status: FINAL OWNER-DIRECTED GOVERNANCE BASELINE

## Core principle

AI != Financial Truth.

Deterministic financial/data/evaluation services and Evidence remain
authoritative.

## AI may

Interpret validated evidence; generate hypotheses; plan bounded
research; call approved tools; delegate bounded specialist tasks;
compare results; synthesize learning; generate next hypotheses; provide
advisory outputs.

## AI may not

Trade, send broker orders, mutate production without human approval,
bypass gates, self-escalate permissions, self-modify budgets,
auto-promote research, declare LIVE without verified Data Trust, treat
untrusted content as executable, or create self-modifying production
logic.

## Governance chain

Human Authority → Thin Governor / Policy Gate → Orchestrator →
Agents/Tools → Deterministic Truth Services → Evidence/Memory/Version →
Advisory Result → Human Approval.

## Run provenance

Every governed run must bind, as applicable: - run_id - parent_run_id -
hypothesis_id - dataset_version - code_commit - model_version -
prompt_version - evaluation_version - policy_version - approval_state -
timestamps - artifact references

Missing required provenance blocks governed acceptance.

## Governor

Owns authorization, transition policy, run identity, provenance binding,
budget/quota and audit integration.

Must not duplicate financial logic or existing safety guards.

## Orchestrator

Owns decomposition, bounded delegation, specialist selection, bounded
parallelism, synthesis and termination.

Does not own financial truth.

## Specialist

Must have purpose, capability, allowed tools, schemas, authority
boundary, budget/timeout, trace identity and failure behavior.

## G2

Governance G2 is VERIFIED. Runtime G2 is NOT YET IMPLEMENTED.

G2 blocks all real-data AI-2 runs until independent validation and owner
acceptance.

## Memory

Episodic research evidence is immutable. Semantic learning is versioned
and curated. No memory operation may silently modify production logic.

## External intelligence

Untrusted by default. Before AI-6: provenance, source verification,
injection defense, tool boundary, Two-Key Rule and human escalation.

## Autonomy levels

L0 Observer L1 Assistant L2 Bounded Research Agent L3 Orchestrator L4
Consequential Action --- human-controlled only in current roadmap

Agents cannot silently increase their autonomy level.

## Security

Secrets, credentials and private keys are never opened, copied,
displayed or hashed during forensic work.

## Acceptance

Governed AI requires source/test/runtime evidence as applicable,
provenance, failure-path evidence, independent validation where required
and an approval record.

## Binding additive owner direction — 2026-10-02

### Continuous market and web intelligence
The product must target scheduled and event-triggered monitoring across an explicit, versioned, auditable source universe, not only user-requested prompts. The source universe may expand over time, but the product must report what is covered, what is stale, what failed and what is not covered. “All web” is a product ambition translated into a governed source-coverage system; it is not a literal guarantee of universal crawling.

### Fundamental data integrity
Financial statement values and company events must remain source-grounded, point-in-time and versioned. Preserve publication time, reporting period, units/currency, restatements, auditor status, source tier and unresolved conflicts. Primary sources are preferred when available; secondary sources may corroborate or provide context but may not silently overwrite primary evidence. AI may explain facts and uncertainty, not manufacture or silently reconcile them.

### Product Intelligence and recurring audits
The system may inspect privacy-safe product telemetry and run scheduled evaluations of filters, strategies, AI quality, data trust, providers, operations, reliability, security and UX. Audit cadence and budgets must be configured, observable and stoppable. Recommendations must include evidence, baseline, expected impact, uncertainty, risk and rollback/evaluation plan. No automated production mutation, release, filter promotion, permission change or budget change.

### Reference behavior and improvement claims
TabloKhani is an initial market-board interpretation benchmark only. Reproduction of reference behavior must be lawful and documented. Claims that the product exceeds reference sites require versioned benchmark evidence, not model assertion or marketing language.

### Preserved gates
G2 remains mandatory before real-data AI-2 execution. External intelligence remains untrusted by default and requires provenance, verification, injection defense, approved tools and Two-Key Rule. Synthetic is not LIVE. Monitoring specification is not proof of live monitoring. No production mutation without human approval.
