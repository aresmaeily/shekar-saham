## 4. Activation (decision 10)

Activation is a RUNTIME, audited kill-switch transition executed only AFTER the full battery passes: `GLOBAL → RELEASED` and the two approved job switches (`OA-ALERT-OPS-DISPATCHER`, `OA-ALERT-OPS-FRESHNESS-30MIN`) → RELEASED, each recorded in `alert_ops_audit` (`KILL_SWITCH_TRANSITION`, actor recorded, from→to). Code and schema defaults remain ENGAGED (fail-closed) for every fresh deployment; the transition is the owner pre-authorization of decision 5 executed under decision 10. No external delivery or live-data operation is enabled by activation — the channel set is closed in the type system.

## 5. Boundary statement

What this capability does NOT do (all structurally enforced): no Telegram/Bale/webhook/email/SMS/WebSocket-fanout delivery; no live-provider calls or real-data scans; no web crawling or external source discovery; no trading or production-strategy mutation; no AI-2 real-data execution (`AI2_ENABLED=0`, `verified=False`, `SYNTHETIC_OR_PAPER` unchanged); no digest generation; no per-kind budget-override system; no credential handling of any kind.
