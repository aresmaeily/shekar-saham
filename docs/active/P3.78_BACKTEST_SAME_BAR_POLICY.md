# P3.78 — Conservative same-bar TP/SL

Date: 2026-08-15

Close-only bars still compare **close** to TP then SL (a single close cannot hit both).

When a bar supplies high and low and both TP and SL are reachable, the engine now takes a **conservative stop** (`stop_loss_same_bar_ambiguous`) instead of assuming the take-profit.

Walk-forward remains rolling out-of-sample validation of a **fixed** filter. It is not a parameter optimizer. Train indexes stay excluded from each test slice.

Starlette/httpx2 deprecation is test-only. Dependencies were not upgraded.
